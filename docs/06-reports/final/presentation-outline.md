## Slide guidelines

- One message per slide. The title states the message ("The node caught 89% of diseased leaves"), not just the topic ("Detection").
- Use figures, photos and short videos over text. No more than about 20 words of body text per slide.
- Every number has a unit and context (how many trials, what conditions).
- Use large fonts (at least 24 pt) and check the slides are readable from the back of the room.
- Embed videos and also keep a local copy. Never rely on venue Wi-Fi.
- Rehearse with a timer at least twice. The second run should be within the time limit.
- Prepare answers for the questions you are dreading. They are usually the ones you get.
